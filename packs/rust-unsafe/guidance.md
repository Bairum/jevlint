Judge only the primary source item. Supplied types, callees and parent source are evidence, not additional finding targets. Report a concrete locally visible contract contradiction and its reachable input or state transition; do not infer whole-program soundness from a pass.

Use actual API contracts and visible allocation, initialization, reference-count and cleanup dataflow. An unsafe signature or SAFETY comment does not excuse an internally contradicted requirement, but a genuinely delegated and otherwise uncontradicted unsafe caller obligation is not a finding. Raw pointers, casts, padding and raw MaybeUninit storage alone are not defects.

Do not invent constructors, destructors, foreign ownership contracts, macro expansions or unseen callback guarantees. Pass when sufficient local evidence or an explicit exception establishes compliance. For rules requiring an external contract, absent contract evidence alone is not a failure. Otherwise abstain when the subject is present but necessary facts are missing and neither compliance nor a concrete violation can be established. Tests are excluded by default. These are optional semantic warnings, not substitutes for rustc, Clippy, Miri, sanitizers or human review.
